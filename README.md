# Çetin Boya — Industrial Paint & Coatings Website
A corporate website for **Çetin Boya**, a Turkish industrial paint and coatings supplier with 30 years in the sector, offering paint solutions across automotive, general industrial, marine, and heavy industry applications.

## 🌐 LIVE SITE
https://xn--etin-zoa.eliud-muwowo.com/

## ABOUT
Çetin Boya positions itself under the tagline **"Her Yüzeyde İzimiz Var"** ("Our mark is on every surface") — offering end-to-end paint solutions for production processes, from automotive to marine coatings, under one roof. The site highlights the company's fast turnaround, wide distribution network across Istanbul and Kocaeli, partnerships with leading paint manufacturers, and flexible solutions from custom color matching to large-scale industrial projects.
